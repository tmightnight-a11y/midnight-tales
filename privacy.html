Privacy Policy

Midnight Tales  ·  Last updated: April 2026

Short version: We connect to your TikTok account only with your explicit permission, we collect the minimum data necessary to publish content, and we never sell or share your data with third parties.

1. Information We Collect

Midnight Tales accesses your TikTok account only with your explicit permission through TikTok's official OAuth 2.0 authorization flow. We collect and store only the minimum data required to operate the service:

  • Your TikTok account identifier (user ID)
  • OAuth access token and refresh token, used solely to publish videos on your behalf
  • Your TikTok display name and handle, used to display account information in the dashboard

We do not collect your TikTok password, private messages, follower lists, or any data beyond what is required to publish content.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to:

  • Authenticate with TikTok on your behalf
  • Publish AI-generated video content to your authorised TikTok account
  • Display your account information within the Midnight Tales dashboard

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, analytics sold to third parties, or any purpose beyond the core publishing functionality.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, trade, rent, or distribute your personal information to any third parties under any circumstances. Your data is used exclusively to provide the Midnight Tales service to you.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your authentication tokens are stored securely and are encrypted at rest. We implement industry-standard security practices to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

We do not store your TikTok password or any credential beyond the OAuth tokens issued by TikTok's authorization server.

5. Data Retention

Your data is retained only as long as necessary to provide the service. If you disconnect your TikTok account or request deletion, all associated tokens and account identifiers are permanently deleted from our systems within 30 days.

6. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Revoke access: disconnect Midnight Tales from your TikTok account at any time via TikTok settings → Manage app permissions
  • Data deletion: request deletion of all your data by contacting us at tmightnight@gmail.com
  • Data portability: request a copy of the data we hold about you

7. TikTok's Privacy Policy

When you connect your TikTok account, you are also subject to TikTok's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. We encourage you to review TikTok's policies at tiktok.com.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. We will notify users of material changes where practically possible.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests related to this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at tmightnight@gmail.com.
