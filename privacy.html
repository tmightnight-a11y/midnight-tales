Privacy Policy

Midnight Tales

Last updated: April 2026

1. Information We Collect

Midnight Tales accesses your TikTok account only with your explicit permission through TikTok's official OAuth 2.0 authorization. We collect and store only the minimum data required to publish content on your behalf, including your access token and account identifier.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to authenticate with TikTok and publish video content to your authorized account. We do not use your data for any other purpose.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, trade, or distribute your personal information to any third parties.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your authentication tokens are stored securely on your local device. We do not store your data on external servers.

5. Data Retention

Your data is retained only as long as necessary to provide the service. You can revoke access at any time through your TikTok account settings.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to revoke the App's access to your TikTok account at any time. Upon revocation, all stored tokens are invalidated.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact Us
